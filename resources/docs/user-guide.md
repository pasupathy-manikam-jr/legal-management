Advocate runs a law firm's day-to-day work in one place: cases, hearings, clients, time and billing, documents, research and compliance. This guide walks through each area in the order it appears in the sidebar.

## Getting started

Sign in with your email and password; everything else is reached from the sidebar on the left.

### Signing in

1. Open the Advocate address your firm gave you. It takes you to **Log in to your account**.
2. Enter your **Email address** and **Password**. Tick **Remember me** to stay signed in on this device.
3. Forgot it? Choose **Forgot password?** and follow the link sent to your email.

After five wrong passwords for the same email, sign-in pauses for about a minute.

### The screen

- **Sidebar**: every area of the app, grouped by topic (Overview, Case & Legal Operations, Billing and so on). Click a group heading to open it; opening one closes the last, so only one is open at a time. The menu stays where it is when you move between pages.
- **Search menu…**: type at the top of the sidebar to filter the menu to matching items.
- **Your name** (bottom of the sidebar): opens a menu with **Settings** (your own account), **System Settings** and **Log out**.
- **User Guide**: this page, under Overview in the sidebar and on the dashboard.

### Lists, filters and tables

Most pages share the same controls:

- **Tabs** along the top split the list (for example All, Active, Closed). The count on each tab reflects your current search and filters.
- **Search and filters** sit on one line above the table. **Clear Filters** resets them.
- **Column headings** with arrows sort the table; click again to reverse.
- **Rows per page** (10, 25, 50 or 100) and page numbers are at the bottom.
- **Add** buttons open a form in a pop-up window. Edit and delete are on each row.
- **Deleting** always asks you to confirm first. The focus starts on **Cancel**, so pressing Enter never deletes by accident.

### Light and dark themes

Go to your name → **Settings** → **Appearance** and choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System** (follows your computer).

## Dashboard, calendar and reports

These three pages show the state of the practice at a glance; you don't enter anything here.

### Dashboard

The first page after signing in. It shows:

- **Headline figures**: Active Cases, Active Clients, Pending Tasks and Total Revenue.
- **Upcoming Hearings**: scheduled court hearings, soonest first.
- **Collections**: what has been invoiced, collected, is outstanding or overdue, plus time that is ready to bill.
- **Monthly Revenue**: payments received per month. Pick another year from the dropdown beside the chart.
- **Tasks by Priority** and **Recent Tasks**.

**Refresh** at the top reloads the figures.

### Calendar

Hearings (red), tasks due (amber) and case timeline events (purple) on one calendar.

- Switch between **Month**, **Week** and **Day** views.
- **Previous** and **Next** move through time; **Today** jumps back.
- The side panel lists **Upcoming Events** and totals for the period.

### Analytics & Reports

Practice-wide trends: cases by year, the closure rate and average time to resolve a case, billable hours, the collection rate and yearly revenue. It also flags overdue invoices and lists recent cases, with **View All** to go to the full list.

## Cases and hearings

A case holds everything about one matter: its client, court, team, timeline, hearings, tasks, documents and time. Find it under **Case Management → Cases**.

### Opening a case

1. Go to **Cases** and choose **Add Case**.
2. Fill in the **Title**, **Client**, **Case type**, **Status** and **Priority** (Low, Medium or High).
3. Add what you know: **Court**, **Judge**, **Filing date**, **Opposing party** and **Opposing counsel**, **Lead lawyer**, **Hourly rate**, **Expected completion** and a **Description**.
4. Save. The case appears in the list.

Filter the list by priority tab, status, type or court, or search by name. **View** opens the case.

### Working on a case

The case page has a **Details** panel and these tabs:

| Tab | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| Timeline & notes | **Add timeline entry**: record a timeline event or an internal note, with the date it happened. |
| Hearings | See every hearing on this case. |
| Tasks | See the case's tasks, their assignee and status. |
| Documents | **Upload document** and set its confidentiality (Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted). |
| Time | Time logged on the case, split into billed and unbilled. |
| Team | **Add team member** with a role: Lead, Associate or Paralegal. |

### Hearings

**Case Management → Hearings** lists every hearing across all cases.

1. Choose **Add Hearing**, pick the **Case**, then set the **Title**, **Type**, **Court**, **Judge**, **Date & time** and **Duration (minutes)**.
2. After the hearing, edit it to set the **Status** (Scheduled, In Progress, Completed, Postponed or Cancelled) and record the **Outcome**.

**Quick Filters** jump to Yesterday, Today or Tomorrow. **View case** opens the hearing's case.

## Courts and judges

Keep a directory of the courts and judges you appear before; cases and hearings then pick from it. Find them under **Court Management**.

### Courts

1. Go to **Courts** and choose **Add Court**.
2. Enter the **Court name**, **Court type**, **Jurisdiction**, **Address**, **Phone** and **Email**, and mark it **Active** or **Inactive**.

Switch between **List View** and **Grid View** at the top right. Each court shows how many cases are heard there.

### Judges

1. Go to **Judges** and choose **Add Judge**.
2. Enter the **Name**, **Title** (for example District Judge), **Court**, contact details and any **Notes**, and mark them **Active** or **Inactive**.

Filter judges by court. Each judge shows their number of hearings.

### Hearing Diary

**Court Management → Hearing Diary** is the same hearing list described under Cases and hearings, reached from the court side of the menu.

## Clients and communication

Every case belongs to a client, so add the client first. Find these under **Client & Communication**.

### Clients

1. Go to **Client Management → Clients** and choose **Add Client**.
2. Enter the **Name**, **Type** (for example Individual or Corporate), **Company**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Address** and any **Notes**, and set the **Status** to Active or Inactive.

**View** opens the client's page: their contact details, open cases, invoices (invoiced and outstanding totals), billed time and recent communication.

### Client documents

**Client Management → Documents** lists documents filed against clients.

1. Choose **Add Document**, then pick the **Client** and optionally a **Case** (or leave it as not case related).
2. Choose the file, then set the **Type**, **Stage** (Draft, Review or Final) and **Confidentiality** (Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted).

Filter by client, type or the Active and Archived tabs. **Download** saves the file.

### Communication

**Communication** keeps conversations with clients and between team members, like a chat.

1. Choose **New conversation** and pick who it is with: a **Client** or a **Team member**.
2. Type in **Write a message…** and send.

Search old conversations with **Search conversations…**. **Delete conversation** removes a whole thread.

## Billing

Billing runs in one direction: log time and expenses against a case, raise an invoice from the unbilled time, then record payments against the invoice. Find it under **Billing & Invoicing**.

1. Log time and expenses against a case.
2. **Add Invoice** for the case.
3. **Mark as sent** when it goes to the client.
4. **Record payment** as money comes in.
5. The invoice becomes **Paid** once payments cover it.

### Time Sheet

The **Time Sheet** shows a week of logged time per team member.

1. Choose **Add Time Sheet**, pick the **Case**, then enter the **Date**, **Minutes**, **Rate / hour** and a **Description**.
2. Tick **Billable** if the client pays for it.

Move between weeks with **Previous week** and **Next week**, and filter by member. Each entry shows whether it is logged but not yet billed, partly billed or billed on an invoice.

### Expenses

1. Choose **Add Expense**, pick the **Case** and **Category**, then enter the **Date**, **Amount** and **Description**.
2. Mark it **Billable to client** or **Firm overhead**.

New expenses are **Pending**. Use **Approve** or **Reject** on each row. The cards at the top total all, pending and approved expenses.

### Invoices

1. Choose **Add Invoice** and pick the **Case**. The invoice collects all of that case's unbilled billable time.
2. Set the **Invoice date**, **Due date**, **Tax %** and any **Notes**.
3. When it goes to the client, choose **Mark as sent**.

An invoice is Draft, Sent, Paid or Cancelled. A sent invoice past its due date shows as **Overdue** automatically. Cancelling an invoice releases its time so it can be billed again. **Copy link** copies the invoice's address for a colleague; **View** opens it with its lines, payments and balance.

### Payments

1. Open an invoice and choose **Record payment**, or choose **Add Payment** on the Payments page and select the invoice.
2. Enter the **Amount**, **Date**, **Method** (Cash, Check, Bank, Credit Card or Online Payment) and a **Reference**.

When payments cover the total, the invoice becomes **Paid**.

## Documents and media

The document library holds the firm's documents with a full version history; the media library holds images and other files. Find them under **Documents & Media**.

### The document library

**Document Management → Documents** shows each document as a folder tile. The tabs split them into All, Draft, Review, Final and Archived; filter further by category or confidentiality level, or search.

### Uploading a document

1. Choose **Upload Document**.
2. Enter the **Name** and a **Description**, pick the **Category**, then use **Select File** to choose the file.
3. Set the **Status** (Draft, Review, Final or Archived) and **Confidentiality** (Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted), and add **Tags** separated by commas.
4. Save. The document starts at version v1.0.

### Versions

Click a folder tile to open the document. It shows every version with its date, and a details panel.

- **Add Version** uploads a new file. It becomes the current version; earlier ones are kept.
- On any version: open it in the browser, **Download** it, **Make current** to roll back, or **Delete version**. The current version can't be deleted.
- **Edit Details** changes the name, client, case, category, status, confidentiality, tags and description.
- **Delete document** removes the document and all its versions.

### Media Library

**Media Library** stores images and files you want to reuse, such as logos and scans.

1. Choose **Upload Media**, give it a **Title**, pick a **Folder** and choose the **File**.
2. Search with **Search media files…**; use **Download** or **Delete** on each item.

## Legal research

Track research work, keep the firm's know-how and record the case law you rely on. Find these under **Legal Research**.

| Page | What it's for | Key fields |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research Projects | A piece of research, usually for a case | Title, Research question, Case, Type, Priority, Lead, Due Date, Status (Active, On Hold, Completed, Cancelled) |
| Knowledge Articles | Internal guides and know-how | Title, Summary, Body, Category, Tags, Status (Draft, Published, Archived) |
| Legal Precedents | Case law and how much it matters | Case name, Citation, Court, Jurisdiction, Decision date, Holding, Relevance (0–100), Case, Status (Active, Questioned, Overruled) |

On each page, **Add Research Project**, **New Article** or **Add Legal Precedent** opens the form. On projects and precedents, the status button (**Move to the next status**) moves a row along without opening it, and **View case** jumps to the linked case. Research Projects also has **List View** and **Grid View**.

## Compliance

Keep the firm's regulatory obligations, audits, risks and professional licences in one place. Find these under **Compliance & Regulatory**; each page has an **Add** button for a new record.

| Page | What it's for | Key fields |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compliance Requirements | Obligations the firm must meet | Title, What is required, Category, Frequency, Priority, Owner, Deadline, Last reviewed, Status (Pending, In Progress, Compliant, Non Compliant) |
| Compliance Audits | Internal or external audits | Audit title, Type, Auditor, Audit date, Risk level, Findings, Completion, Status (Planned, In Progress, Completed, Cancelled) |
| Risk Assessments | Risks to the firm or a case | Risk title, Category, Case or firm-wide, Probability, Impact, Mitigation, Responsible, Next review, Status (Open, Closed) |
| Professional Licenses | Each lawyer's practising licences | Holder, Licence type, Licence number, Issuing authority, Jurisdiction, Issued, Expires, Status |
| CLE Tracking | Continuing legal education | User, Course / Provider, Category, Credits earned, Credits required, Completion date, Certificate URL |
| Regulatory Bodies | The bar associations and regulators you deal with | Name, Short name, Type, Jurisdiction, Website, Contact email and phone |

A few things happen automatically:

- A requirement past its deadline shows as **Overdue**.
- A licence past its expiry date shows as **Expired**. Use **Renew for another term** to extend it.
- Risk Assessments scores each risk as probability × impact and plots it on the **Risk Matrix**.
- On requirements, **Move to next status** steps a row forward without opening it.

## Tasks

Tasks are the to-do list for the firm, usually tied to a case. Find them under **Task & Workflow → Tasks**.

1. Choose **Add Task** and enter the **Title**, **Type**, **Case** (or no case), **Assignee**, **Priority**, **Due date** and any **Notes**.
2. Set the **Status** as the work moves along.

The page header counts open and overdue tasks. Switch between **List View** and **Kanban View**; on the board, drag a card to another column to change its status. Filter by status, type or user, or search.

## Setup lists

The dropdowns across the app (case types, statuses, categories and so on) come from lists you can edit. Each area of the menu has a **Setup** group holding its lists.

| Menu group | Lists |
| --- | --- |
| Case Setup | Case Types, Case Status, Event Types, Hearing Types |
| Court Setup | Court Types |
| Client Setup | Client Types, Document Types |
| Research Setup | Research Types, Practice Areas, Research Categories, Research Sources |
| Compliance Setup | Compliance Categories, Compliance Frequencies, Risk Categories, Compliance Audit Types |
| Task Setup | Task Types, Task Status |
| Billing Setup | Expense Categories |
| Document Setup | Categories (the same list as Document Types) |

To add an entry, open the list, choose **Add**, and give it a name and **Description**. Some lists also take a **Color** for their badges, a parent (research categories sit under a practice area) or a link (research sources, for example https://westlaw.com). Mark an entry **Inactive** to hide it from new records without losing it from old ones.

## Administration

The **System Control** group is mostly for administrators. Anyone can view Company Profile and Notification Templates, but only administrators can change them. Members, Roles and Settings open for administrators only. **My Account** is for everyone.

### Company Profile

**Advocate → Company Profile** holds the firm's public details. Choose **Edit Profile** to change them. There are three groups:

- **Personal & Contact Details**: advocate name, email, phone, address, website.
- **Professional Details**: bar registration number, law degree, university, years of experience, specialization, court jurisdictions, languages, notable cases, success rate.
- **Business & Firm Details**: firm name, business type, registration number, practice size, established, office hours, consultation fees, services offered.

### Team members

**Team Members → Members** lists everyone who can sign in.

1. Choose **Add User**, then enter the **Name**, **Email**, **Job title**, **Role** and a **Password**.
2. The new member signs in with that email and password.

On each member you can **Edit**, **Reset password**, switch them between **Active** and **Suspended** (a suspended member can't sign in) or **Delete** them. The firm always keeps at least one active administrator. Each member shows their lead cases and time logged this month. Switch between **List View** and **Grid View**.

### Roles

**Team Members → Roles** defines roles such as Senior Associate, each with a **Description** and a set of **Permissions** grouped by area. Built-in system roles can't be deleted.

Today only the administrator role actually restricts access. The permission ticks are recorded, but not yet enforced page by page.

### Notification Templates

**Notification Templates** holds the wording of the firm's notifications for email, Slack and Twilio (SMS): a **Name**, a **Subject** (email only) and the **Message**. Turn a template **Active** or **Off**. Templates can be edited now; the app doesn't send them yet.

### Settings

**Settings** (System Settings) holds firm-wide configuration, one section each:

| Section | What it sets |
| --- | --- |
| System Settings | Default language and timezone, date and time formats |
| Brand Settings | Firm name, title text, footer text and theme colour |
| Currency Settings | Default currency, symbol and its position, decimal places, and separators, for example 1,234.56 or 1.234,56 |
| Email Settings | The outgoing mail server, with **Test Email Configuration** |
| Email Notifications | Which notification templates are switched on |
| Slack Settings | The Slack connection, with **Test Slack Configuration** |
| Twilio Settings | SMS sending, with **Test SMS Configuration** |
| Payment Settings | Stripe (Sandbox or Live, with **Verify Stripe Key**), PayPal and bank transfer details |
| Google Calendar Settings | The calendar start day and a **Subscription Link** you add to Google Calendar under Other calendars → From URL |

### My Account

Everyone has **My Account** (also your name → **Settings**):

- **Profile**: change your name and email, or delete your account.
- **Password**: change your password.
- **Appearance**: Light, Dark or System theme.
